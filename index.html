A small dungeon stands between you and the exit. Each room holds a boss that never moves, camped in the farthest chamber. Every 7 turns it summons a minion that hunts you down. Move next to an enemy and it auto-targets — if more than one is adjacent, tap the one you want to hit instead. Type the correct answer to strike. Minions fall in one hit; the boss takes three. Move (N/S/E/W) to close the distance or dodge, or hold your ground — one action per turn. Three hits on you and it's over.
